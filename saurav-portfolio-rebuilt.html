03 — Scripts

The stories that live on paper.

Nine pieces in three languages. Some tragic, some absurd, and some sitting uncomfortably between the two.

01

Terah Din Thirteen days of funeral rites, and what grief is billed at

Screenplay · Bhojpuri. Babuji chal gahilan magar karza chhod gahilan. Ab dekha, humnike kekar terhavi manawake — garibi ka, ya aapan? An old man dies at dawn and the family has thirteen days to bury him properly.

Short
02

The Last Shift Seven men sent to seal a radioactive breach

Short fiction · English. Told in fourteen numbered days, one name crossed off the board at a time. The men were chosen for the job because nobody would come asking after them.

Prose
03

Mrit Aur Mrityu A man meets Death, who turns out to know less than he does

Screenplay · Hindi. A two-hander that runs on one idea: Death has no answers, no scales, possibly no hands, and has never once been asked what it wants.

Short
04

Abhi Bhi Yahin Hoon A father talks into the dark, and one night it answers

Screenplay · Hindi. After a crash, a father needs to be absolved and his son cannot do it — not out of anger, but because the memory that would make absolution possible isn't there.

Short
05

Lifafa Two brothers and their father's last letter

Screenplay · Hindi. A sealed envelope on a desk between two men, neither of whom will let go of it. The argument is never really about the letter.

Short
06

The Certificate A builder, an engineer, and two per cent

Screenplay · English. Set in a site office at a half-built government school. Three to four minutes, two actors, one table.

Short
07

Sawaal A father, a minister, and a leaked exam paper

Screenplay · Hindi. No cameras, no press conference. A man who lost a son to an institution's convenience asks the one question nobody made the institution answer.

Short
08

Broke Kingdom A bankrupt monarchy, in four acts

Comedy · English. The king threw the kingdom's grain into fountains for his birthday, the princess gambled the last tiara on a squirrel race, and the revolutionary at the gate has a legitimate grievance and an illegitimate birth.

Comedy
09

Planned & Prepared A man empties a pharmacy before anyone knows why

Comedy · English. Ten minutes of deadpan between a hypochondriac in an ill-fitting PPE suit and a bored pharmacy clerk. The joke runs the whole script.

05 — About

The person behind the words.

I'm a senior content writer with a business management background and about five years of writing behind me, three and a half of them in edtech.

Most of that time has been spent on one audience: parents trying to make an expensive, irreversible decision about a child's schooling, usually with incomplete information and a deadline.

The fiction is the same preoccupation in another form. A priest itemising funeral rites for a family that can't afford them is the fee report with the numbers taken out. A bankrupt king throwing grain into fountains is the same thing played for laughs.
